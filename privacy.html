Privacy Policy

Daily Finds · last updated 2026-11-19

This page explains what happens with your data when you react to the product videos of @fayoz8646 on Instagram or open this product page.

Comments and direct messages

If you comment on one of our Instagram posts or reels with the keyword shown in the video (or the word “link”), our tool uses Instagram's official API to send you one private reply with a “Send the link” button. If you tap it (or reply), it sends the product link in the same chat. Instagram gives the tool the comment ID, its text and time, and the commenter's account ID and username; they are used only to decide whether to reply and to skip our own comments. To know that you tapped the button the tool checks only who sent the latest message in that chat and when — it never reads your messages. We do not store your name, username, profile or comment text. While we wait for your tap (at most 7 days) we keep the Instagram-scoped ID of the chat so the link can be sent; to avoid sending the same link twice we keep a one-way hashed (non-reversible) identifier for up to 14 days. Both are then deleted. We never send other messages, follow requests or advertising.

Affiliate links

Product links are affiliate links (Admitad network, AliExpress store). If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you open a link, the store and the affiliate network may use cookies under their own privacy policies.

This page

The product page is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, ads or cookies of its own. Product images load from the store's image servers; GitHub and those servers may log basic technical data (such as your IP address) under their own policies.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Comments and messages are processed by Instagram (Meta) under Instagram's terms.

Data deletion

You can delete your comment at any time. To ask us to delete any data related to you, send a direct message to @fayoz8646 on Instagram with the words “delete my data”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm. Because we keep identifiers for at most 14 days, usually nothing remains to delete.

Contact

Direct message to @fayoz8646 on Instagram.
